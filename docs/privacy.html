Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Abue Studio ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Abue to publish his own videos to his own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (user ID, username, avatar), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account.
  • Public information about the creator's own Twitch videos (VOD list and video files) to create clips.

How we use it

Only to create clips from the creator's own streams and publish them to the creator's own accounts. We do not sell, share or transfer data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the creator's own computer and are never shared. Video files are stored locally and deleted after publishing.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from the settings of TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Twitch. On request, all stored data is deleted within 7 days.

Abue Studio

Abue Studio is a personal publishing tool used by the creator Abue (Twitch: abuegod, TikTok: @elaueloo) to turn his own Twitch streams into short vertical clips and publish them to his own TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts.

Abue Studio es una herramienta personal del creador Abue para convertir sus propias transmisiones de Twitch en clips verticales y publicarlos en sus propias cuentas de TikTok, Instagram y Facebook.

  • It only accesses accounts that the creator connects himself through official login (OAuth).
  • It only publishes content created by the account owner.
  • It does not post comments, likes or follows on any platform.
